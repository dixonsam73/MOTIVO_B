# Connected invitations — agreed direction

**2026-09-17. DIRECTION OF RECORD. NOT SCOPED, NOT APPROVED FOR IMPLEMENTATION, NOT BUILT.**

## The feature

**Invitations are a STANDALONE feature for ALL Connected members, adults included.** They
are **not** a teen mechanism — see §3.

They serve two cases:

1. **an existing Connected member** the inviter knows; and
2. **someone who is not on Études yet**, and must install and join first.

**Entry points, agreed:**

| | Where | Label |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | **People**, near search | **"Invite someone you know"** |
| **Secondary** | **Profile → Account** | **"Invite someone to Études"** |

**Both use the SAME flow and the same share sheet.**

## Boundaries

- **Joining and purchase remain separate.** An invitation does not enrol anyone, and does
  not buy or confer Connected.
- **Per-direction follow approval remains separate and unchanged.** The person being
  followed still approves, and may decline or revoke.
- **An invitation NEVER automatically grants access** — it creates only the opportunity to ask.
- **Études does not read contacts.** Sharing goes through the iOS share sheet.
- **Shared ensembles are OUT OF SCOPE.**
